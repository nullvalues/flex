# flex — Cold-Eyes Review (CER) Backlog

*Last updated: 2026-07-30*

This file is the structured triage log for findings from external cold-eyes reviews.
Each finding is assigned to one quadrant. Findings are not deleted — a resolved
finding keeps its row and gains a marker annotation of the form
`**RESOLVED <phase or checkpoint> — <what landed>**` (or `**SUPERSEDED by
CER-NNN**`). The marker must begin its annotation segment — at the start of the
finding text, right after a sentence-ending `.`/`;`/`:`/em-dash plus a space, or
inside an emphasis/bracket opener (`**`, `(`, `[`) — and its case does not
matter (`RESOLVED`, `Resolved` and `resolved` are equivalent). The `cer-do-now`
checkpoint guard reads exactly this form (`cer.is_resolution_marked`,
INFRA-322/CER-130): a keyword appearing mid-clause, such as `UNRESOLVED` or
`should be resolved`, is never read as a marker, so a genuinely open finding
cannot be waved through a checkpoint by accident.
